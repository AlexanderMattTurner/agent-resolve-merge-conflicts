- `claude-run`'s credential ladder is now rendered from `lib_credential_ladder.rungs()`, the table the resolve job already walks. Its inputs are `rung_1` .. `rung_8`, positional, so a caller decides which secret fills which slot. The free same-credential repeat moves from after the last rung to rung 2, where it re-spends the metered rung's credential on a proven zero-cost failure.
- The ladder's waits escalate 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70 seconds. `claude-run` waited 5 to 75 and the table declared 10 to 90; the one table now sets both.

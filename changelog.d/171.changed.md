- The merge-delta reviewer judges a regenerated lockfile by its manifest rather than its bytes. Asking it to prove bytes that match neither parent refused every lockfile conflict there is, since a regenerated lockfile matches neither by construction.
- The reviewer scores a REMOVAL by effect. When both parents fix one defect in their own words, the survivor carries the loser's fix under different wording, and matching the removed text alone read a correct resolution as a revert.
